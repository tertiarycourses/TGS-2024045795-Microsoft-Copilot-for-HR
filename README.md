## About the course

Practise grounded prompting, HR evidence review, policy knowledge, and governed agents with synthetic records. People retain responsibility for hiring, employee service, approvals, privacy and fairness.

## Learning outcomes

1. Compare how generative and agentic AI reshape HR operating models versus conventional HR delivery.
2. Analyse where Microsoft 365 Copilot and AI agents can improve end-to-end HR processes and employee experience.
3. Conduct feasibility analysis and design a governed implementation for an HR generative-AI or agentic-AI use case.
4. Apply legal, ethical, privacy, security, fairness and human-oversight controls to AI-enabled HR work.

## Topics covered

- **Unit 1: From Generative AI to Agentic HR** — Choose the lightest capability that safely solves the work
- **Unit 2: Microsoft 365 Copilot Across the HR Lifecycle** — Generate and analyse with a human in control
- **Unit 3: Build Governed HR Agents** — Agent Builder for knowledge; Copilot Studio for orchestration
- **Unit 4: Responsible Adoption and HR Transformation** — Govern decisions, data, people and agent operations
